The Table is a forum for community members to meet and create systems and solutions specifically for the potentail of an extended grid down situation.

Have you ever considered the effect, to human life, if a
power outage persisted over a week or a month? Who
do you know in the Valley that would be affected and how? 

What is the time frame until
suffering or death? 

We’ve all gone a day or two without electricity. Most of us are mildly
hindered in accomplishing daily concerns. 

Considered as camping, the
issue is initially small.
 
That is perhaps a week. 

As the power down scenario persists, the injuries that ensue begin to
snowball, life-threatening scenarios accumulate and a mycelial mass of
unexpected yet interconnected issues begins an exponential expansion.
This is a tipping point where the issues metastasize and become
uncontrollable. Had there been a system, due diligence of local
preparedness we would have capped Pandora’s box and kept a control
valve on genuine loss of life.

Businesses may lose revenue and product but the most vulnerable of us,
family members, friends, co-workers could (and will) lose life.
